OriginAI-assisted numerical search of this atlas: start from the proven degree-10, 24-node PI rule CA-T2-10-24-PI-MPC, continue at degree 9, then remove five nodes successively. At each node count, 12 seeded deletion candidates and three perturbation amplitudes were tested; the chain selected the successful rule with the largest minimum weight and boundary margin. The 19-node result was reached through five removals; a further reduction to 18 nodes did not succeed in this campaign. The weights were then minimised in the L2 norm under all moment equations with a penalty for nodes outside the domain and the symmetry kept (tools/minimize_l2.py); the minimizer identified S3 symmetry of order six.
